## Full description

TenderBase helps South African contractors, suppliers and consultants find
and win government work — from first discovery to submitted bid.

FIND
• Search live tenders from South African public procurement sources
• Filter by province, category, organisation and tender type
• Sort by closing date so the most urgent opportunities surface first
• Zero setup: open the app and browse what's open right now

TRACK
• Save tenders to a personal deadline command centre
• Get alerts when new tenders match your saved searches
• Deadline reminders for tenders closing within 48 hours
• Swipe to save or hide — keep your feed focused

PREPARE
• A bid workspace for every saved tender: checklist and notes
• Sensible default checklist: register, briefing, documents, pricing, submit
• Share a complete bid pack with your team as plain text
• Documents grouped by type — tender notices, addenda and annexures

WORKS OFFLINE
• Cached tenders keep browsing working without signal
• Save searches offline and they sync when you're back online
• Workspaces back up to the server and restore on a new device

Data covers South African public sector tender publications. TenderBase is an
independent tool and is not affiliated with any government entity.
